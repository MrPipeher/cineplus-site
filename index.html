What it does

Small app. Straightforward playback.

Browse

Trending movies and shows, with seasons and episodes where the source provides them.

Search

Look up a title and open the sources that are available for it.

Your player

Hand playback to the Android player you already use — MX Player, VLC, or the built-in option.

No account

No sign-up, no subscription, and no password to remember.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Find a title

    Browse the trending rows or search for something specific.

  2. 2

    Choose a source

    CinePlus lists the links the source exposes for that title.

  3. 3

    Play

    Pick your player. The video is delivered by the source, not by CinePlus.

About third-party content

CinePlus is a discovery and playback interface. It does not host, store, or upload video files. Titles, links, and streams come from third-party sources and can change or disappear without notice. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to access content in your jurisdiction.

If you believe a link infringes your rights, email support@olgply.xyz with the details and we will review it.
