Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Last updated 24 November 2026

CinePlus is a media discovery and playback app for Android. This policy explains what information the app and the services it uses may handle. It applies to the CinePlus app and this website.

1. No account required

CinePlus does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or payment details to use the app.

2. Information that may be collected

To operate and troubleshoot the app, CinePlus and the third-party services it uses may process:

  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and general region.
  • Network information such as IP address, which is necessarily visible to any service you connect to.
  • Usage and diagnostic information such as which screens or sources were opened, playback errors, and crash logs.
  • Advertising and analytics data processed by third-party advertising, analytics, and player services, under their own privacy policies.

We do not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, or files from your device.

3. Third-party services

CinePlus connects to third-party content sources and can hand playback to third-party video players. Those services may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data independently. Their privacy policies apply to their processing. CinePlus does not control those services.

4. How information is used

  • To provide and operate the app.
  • To diagnose crashes, playback failures, and abuse.
  • To measure basic usage and improve the app.
  • To display advertising where applicable, including through third-party ad services.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the app (hosting, diagnostics, advertising, content and player services), or when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

CinePlus is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

8. Your choices

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. You can also limit ad personalization through your Android device settings. Some third-party services provide their own opt-out mechanisms.

9. International users

The app and its service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: support@olgply.xyz.
